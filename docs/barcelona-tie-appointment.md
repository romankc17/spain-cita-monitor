# Booking the TIE fingerprints appointment — Barcelona

For: fingerprints (*toma de huellas*) after a residence permit has been approved.
This must be done from a normal internet connection in Spain. No VPN.

---

## Before you start — three things must be ready

**1. The permit must already be approved.**
Check the status here: https://sede.administracionespublicas.gob.es/infoext2/
Enter the expediente number. It must say *resuelto favorable*.
(Or send a text saying `EXPE` then a space then the expediente number, to `600 12 43 77`. It is free.)

If it does not say approved, the website will refuse the booking. Wait until it does.

**2. The fee must be paid.** This is form **790 código 012**.
Get it here: https://sede.policia.gob.es/Tasa790_012/
Tick one box only:

| Your situation | Amount |
|---|---|
| First time getting the card | **16,08 €** |
| Renewing the card | 19,30 € |
| Long-term residence card | 21,87 € |
| Family member of a Spanish citizen | 12,00 € |

Print three copies. Pay at any bank. Keep the stamped receipt.

**3. Form EX-17 filled in and signed.**

---

## Have these written down before you open the website

Slots disappear in under a minute, so do not look anything up while booking.

- NIE number — no spaces, no dashes
- Full name, spelled exactly as in the passport, with all surnames in the same order
- Year of birth
- Nationality
- Spanish mobile number (needed for the confirmation code)
- Email address

---

## The website, screen by screen

Open in **Chrome** or **Firefox**, in a **private / incognito window**.
Do **not** let the browser translate the page — it breaks the menus.

**https://icp.administracionelectronica.gob.es/icpplus/index.html**

### Screen 1 — Province
Under `PROVINCIAS DISPONIBLES`, choose **Barcelona**.
Click **Aceptar**.

### Screen 2 — Procedure
Find the section `TRÁMITES CUERPO NACIONAL DE POLICÍA`.
Choose the line that contains the words **TOMA DE HUELLAS**.

The full wording changes from time to time. It will look like one of these:
- `POLICÍA-TOMA DE HUELLAS (EXPEDICIÓN DE TARJETA) INICIAL, RENOVACIÓN, DUPLICADO Y LEY 14/2013`
- `POLICIA - TOMA DE HUELLAS (EXPEDICIÓN DE TARJETA) Y RENOVACIÓN DE TARJETA DE LARGA DURACIÓN`

**Do not** pick these two, they are different things:
- `RECOGIDA DE TARJETA` — that is for collecting the finished card later
- `EXPEDICIÓN DE TARJETAS CUYA AUTORIZACIÓN RESUELVE LA DIRECCIÓN GENERAL DE MIGRACIONES` — only for Ley 14/2013 cases

Click **Aceptar**.

### Screen 3 — Information page
A list of required documents appears. Click **Entrar**.

### Screen 4 — Your details
- Document type: **NIE**
- Number: type it with no spaces and no dashes
- Name: exactly as in the passport
- Year of birth
- Tick the "I am not a robot" box

Click **Aceptar**.

If it says `Datos no encontrados`, try typing the name in CAPITAL LETTERS, and try
with and without the middle name. Spanish systems sometimes join the middle name
onto the surname.

### Screen 5 — Check and continue
Check the name and NIE are correct.
Click **Solicitar Cita**.

### Screen 6 — Choose the office
**Important: for fingerprints you may go to ANY office in Barcelona province.**
It does not have to be where you live.

The city-centre ones are almost always full:
- Rambla Guipúscoa 74
- Gran Via de les Corts Catalanes 584
- Passeig de la Zona Franca 116
- Via Augusta 202

These usually have space, and the card they issue is exactly the same:
- **Badalona** (Carrer de Guifré 263)
- **Terrassa**
- **Sabadell**
- **Cerdanyola del Vallès**
- **Mataró**
- **Granollers**
- **Manresa**

Take whichever has a slot. A train ride is faster than waiting weeks.

Click **Siguiente**.

### Screen 7 — Pick a date and time
If slots exist, choose one.

If it says **`En este momento no hay citas disponibles`** — this is not an error.
It means all slots are taken right now. Close the window, open a new private
window, and try again later. Nothing is broken and nothing is lost.

### Screen 8 — Confirm
Enter the Spanish mobile number and the email address.
A code arrives by SMS. Type it in quickly — you have about 2 minutes.
Click **Confirmar**.

### Screen 9 — SAVE THE PDF IMMEDIATELY
Download and print the confirmation (*justificante*) straight away.
Do not wait for the email.

**This PDF is what gets you through the door.** Without it you will be turned away.

Write down the justificante number. You need it to cancel or look up the booking,
and the phone number and email **cannot be changed online** afterwards.

---

## When to try

There is no official release time — the government does not publish one.
In practice most new slots appear **Monday to Friday, 8:00 to 14:00 Spanish time**.
Cancellations can free up a slot at any hour, including nights and weekends.

Do not press refresh over and over. The site will block you. Try every so often
in a fresh private window instead.

Only one active appointment is allowed per procedure. To change the date you must
cancel the old one first, using **Anular Cita** and the justificante number.

---

## What to bring on the day

- The printed justificante
- Passport, original **and** a photocopy of the photo page
- The approval letter (*resolución favorable*)
- Form EX-17, completed
- The stamped 790-012 fee receipt
- One passport photo, colour, white background
- Empadronamiento certificate, in case they ask

Arrive about **15 minutes early** — not an hour early, they will not let you in.

**Only the person named in the booking will be seen.** They must attend in person
with the original passport.

---

## If something goes wrong

| Message on screen | What it means |
|---|---|
| `En este momento no hay citas disponibles` | No slots left right now. Try again later. Normal. |
| `El usuario ya tiene una cita` | An appointment already exists. Cancel it first. |
| `Datos no encontrados` / `Datos incorrectos` | Name or number does not match the file. Check the spelling. |
| `Solicitud no autorizada` | The permit is not marked approved yet. Wait. |
| `Sesión expirada` | Too slow. Start again from screen 1. |
| Blank page or server error | Site overloaded. Wait a few minutes. |

For technical problems with the website, call **060** (from Spain).
They cannot give you an appointment, only help with faults.

Booking is **free**. Never pay anyone who promises to guarantee a slot.
